The claim
  Hair counts are whole numbers. This film allows 0 through 500 000 inclusive,
  which is 500 001 pigeonholes, and says out loud that 500 000 is a generous
  margin: a typical head is closer to 100–150 thousand. A million-person city
  has at least 1 000 000 people, which is more than 500 001, so two share a count.
  The proof does not name the people or the number.

  A ceiling of “at most 1 000 000 hairs” would need a city of more than
  1 000 001 people. That is why this film does not use that ceiling.
