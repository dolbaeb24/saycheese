Как работает микроволновка
Vertical reel, 1080×1920, 24 fps, hologram-hud style.

Picture
  Drawn in the project: wireframe hologram engine and HUD layer from the
  hologram-hud style of the local film library (copied into engine/), models
  built in code (microwave, water molecule, food bowl). No stock footage, no brands.
  Type: Exo 2 and JetBrains Mono, SIL Open Font License 1.1.
    https://github.com/googlefonts/Exo-2.0  https://github.com/JetBrains/JetBrainsMono

Voice
  Russian narration, voice Kore, Gemini gemini-2.5-flash-preview-tts.
  Pad, HUD sounds, magnetron hum and the final ding are synthesized; no music library.

Facts used
  Household magnetrons work at 2.45 GHz; wavelength c / f ≈ 12.2 cm.
  The door mesh holes (about 1–2 mm) are far smaller than the wavelength, so the waves stay inside.
  Water molecules are electric dipoles; the alternating field keeps turning them,
  and their collisions with neighbours turn that motion into heat (dielectric heating).
  Penetration into wet food is roughly 1–2 cm; deeper layers heat by conduction.
  The turntable moves food through the hot and cold spots of the standing wave.
  The on-screen 75 °C is an illustration, not a measurement.
