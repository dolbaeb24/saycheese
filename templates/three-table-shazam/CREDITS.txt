Как Shazam узнаёт песню в шуме
Vertical reel, 1080×1920, 30 fps.

Picture
  Drawn in the project (Three.js). No stock footage, no Shazam logo.
  Song titles and catalogue numbers on screen are invented.
  Type: Manrope, SIL Open Font License 1.1. https://github.com/sharanda/manrope

Voice
  Russian narration, voice Kore, Gemini gemini-2.5-flash-preview-tts.
  Bed and object sounds are synthesized; no music library.

Source
  Avery Li-Chun Wang, "An Industrial-Strength Audio Search Algorithm", ISMIR 2003.
  Spectrogram peaks (constellation map), pairs of peaks hashed as
  frequency 1 / frequency 2 / time gap, and matching by a consistent time offset.
  The film describes the published method; today's Shazam may differ.
  "Hundreds of keys" and "millions of songs" are orders of magnitude, not figures from the paper.
